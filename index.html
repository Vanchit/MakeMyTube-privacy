EngineerTube Privacy Policy

Privacy information for the EngineerTube Chrome Extension.

EngineerTube is a Chrome extension designed to turn YouTube into a more focused learning and productivity environment. This Privacy Policy explains what information EngineerTube accesses, how it is used, where it is stored, and the choices available to users.

Last updated: August 22, 2026

1. Information We Access and Process

EngineerTube accesses only the information needed to provide its learning, productivity, authentication, and subscription features.

Google Account Information

When you choose to sign in with Google, EngineerTube may receive basic account information provided through Google OAuth, including:

  • Your Google account identifier.
  • Your name.
  • Your email address.
  • Your Google profile picture, when available.

EngineerTube requests only the basic Google identity scopes: openid, email, and profile.

Google account information is used to authenticate you, associate your account with your EngineerTube subscription, and display your signed-in account inside the extension.

YouTube and Learning Activity

EngineerTube processes information from YouTube pages so that features such as Focus Mode, filtering, progress tracking, saved videos, notes, and distraction controls can function.

Depending on the features you use, this may include:

  • Video titles and video identifiers.
  • Channel names.
  • Video URLs.
  • Video duration and playback progress.
  • Watch time.
  • Focus Mode and Normal Mode activity.
  • Saved videos.
  • Timestamped learning notes created by you.
  • Study tags and blocked tags.
  • Trusted channel preferences.
  • Pomodoro session information.
  • Learning progress and productivity statistics.
  • Interactions required to operate EngineerTube features.

This information is used only to provide EngineerTube's learning and productivity functionality.

2. Information Stored Locally

A significant portion of EngineerTube's learning data and preferences is stored locally using Chrome extension storage.

Local data may include:

  • EngineerTube settings.
  • Focus Mode preferences.
  • Study tags.
  • Blocked tags.
  • Trusted channels.
  • Saved learning videos.
  • Timestamped notes.
  • Pomodoro settings and statistics.
  • Watch-time statistics.
  • Progress information.
  • Recommendation-learning preferences.
  • Subscription status cached for the extension interface.

This data is used to remember your preferences and maintain your EngineerTube experience across browser sessions.

3. EngineerTube Backend

EngineerTube uses a secure backend hosted using Cloudflare Workers and related Cloudflare services for account authentication and subscription management.

The backend may store information such as:

  • Your Google account identifier.
  • Your email address.
  • Your name.
  • Your profile picture URL.
  • Your EngineerTube subscription plan.
  • Your subscription status.
  • Your subscription start and expiry information.
  • Razorpay subscription identifiers.
  • Payment event identifiers required to process subscription events.

This information is used only to authenticate users and manage EngineerTube account and subscription access.

4. Payments and Razorpay

EngineerTube Pro subscriptions are processed using Razorpay.

When you choose to purchase EngineerTube Pro, you are redirected to Razorpay's secure payment interface.

EngineerTube does not directly collect or store your credit card number, debit card number, UPI PIN, CVV, banking password, or other sensitive payment credentials.

Razorpay processes payment information according to its own privacy and security policies.

EngineerTube receives only the information necessary to determine subscription status, such as a Razorpay subscription identifier, payment/subscription status, and billing period information.

5. Google Sign-In

EngineerTube uses Google OAuth for authentication.

Google Sign-In is used only to:

  • Verify your identity.
  • Create or identify your EngineerTube account.
  • Display basic account information inside the extension.
  • Associate your account with EngineerTube Pro access.

EngineerTube does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, Google Contacts, or other Google account content.

6. Chrome Extension Permissions

EngineerTube requests Chrome permissions only when they are needed to provide its features.

Storage

Used to save EngineerTube preferences, study settings, learning progress, saved videos, notes, Pomodoro information, and account/subscription state.

Identity

Used to provide Google Sign-In and authenticate your EngineerTube account.

Alarms

Used to support Pomodoro study and break timers even when the extension popup is closed.

Declarative Net Request

Used for EngineerTube's optional advertisement and distraction-reduction functionality through a limited, packaged browser ruleset.

YouTube Host Access

EngineerTube needs access to YouTube pages so it can provide Focus Mode, educational filtering, interface customization, saved-video tools, progress tracking, distraction controls, and other learning features.

EngineerTube API Access

Access to the EngineerTube backend is required for Google authentication and subscription management.

7. How We Use Information

Information accessed by EngineerTube is used only for purposes related to the extension's functionality, including:

  • Authenticating users.
  • Providing Focus Mode.
  • Filtering educational content.
  • Saving user preferences.
  • Providing saved-video and note functionality.
  • Tracking learning and productivity progress.
  • Providing Pomodoro functionality.
  • Managing EngineerTube Pro subscriptions.
  • Maintaining and improving extension reliability.
  • Preventing duplicate or incorrect subscription processing.

8. Data Sharing

EngineerTube does not sell user data.

EngineerTube does not transfer user data to third parties for advertising, creditworthiness, lending, or unrelated purposes.

Information may be processed by service providers only when required to operate EngineerTube, including:

  • Google — for Google OAuth authentication.
  • Cloudflare — for backend infrastructure, database services, networking, and security.
  • Razorpay — for payment and subscription processing.

These providers process information according to their own applicable privacy policies and terms.

9. Advertising

EngineerTube does not use your personal information to create advertising profiles and does not sell your information to advertisers.

EngineerTube may provide features that reduce or hide distracting or advertising-related elements on YouTube, but this does not involve selling or sharing user data for advertising purposes.

10. Remote Code

EngineerTube does not download or execute remote JavaScript or other remotely hosted executable code as part of the extension.

The extension may communicate with external services such as the EngineerTube backend, Google authentication services, and Razorpay, but executable extension logic is packaged with the Chrome extension.

11. Data Retention

Local learning data remains stored in Chrome extension storage until it is cleared, reset, removed through EngineerTube controls, or removed according to Chrome's extension-storage behavior.

Account and subscription information stored by the EngineerTube backend may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Maintain your EngineerTube account.
  • Provide subscription access.
  • Process subscription cancellation and expiration.
  • Prevent duplicate payment-event processing.
  • Meet applicable operational or legal requirements.

12. User Choices and Data Deletion

You can remove or reset many types of locally stored EngineerTube information directly through extension controls, including preferences and learned recommendation information.

You may also uninstall EngineerTube from Chrome at any time.

To request deletion of EngineerTube account information stored by the backend, contact:

13. Security

EngineerTube uses reasonable technical measures designed to protect information handled by the service.

Examples include:

  • HTTPS communication.
  • Google OAuth authentication.
  • Server-side payment API credentials.
  • Razorpay webhook signature verification.
  • Restricted Chrome extension host permissions.
  • Server-side subscription verification.

No online system can guarantee absolute security, but EngineerTube is designed to minimize unnecessary collection and exposure of user information.

14. Children's Privacy

EngineerTube is intended as a general productivity and learning tool and is not specifically directed toward children under the minimum age required to independently use Google accounts, payment services, or Chrome services in their jurisdiction.

15. Third-Party Services

EngineerTube interacts with third-party platforms, including YouTube, Google, Cloudflare, and Razorpay.

Use of those services may also be governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

EngineerTube is an independent browser extension and is not endorsed by or officially affiliated with YouTube or Google unless explicitly stated otherwise.

Material changes will be reflected by updating the Last updated date shown at the top of this page.
