MakeMyTube Privacy Policy

Privacy information for the MakeMyTube Chrome Extension.

MakeMyTube is a Chrome extension that gives users more control over their YouTube experience. It can help shape recommendations, reduce unwanted topics, prioritize selected creators, save videos, create notes, track activity, and provide optional productivity and distraction-control features.

This Privacy Policy explains what information MakeMyTube accesses, how that information is used, where it may be stored, which third-party services are involved, and the choices available to users.

Last updated: August 28, 2026

1. Information We Access and Process

MakeMyTube accesses and processes information only when it is needed to provide extension functionality, authentication, personalization, productivity tools, or subscription services.

Google Account Information

When you choose to sign in with Google, MakeMyTube may receive basic account information provided through Google OAuth, including:

  • Your Google account identifier.
  • Your name.
  • Your email address.
  • Your Google profile picture, when available.

MakeMyTube requests only the basic Google identity scopes: openid, email, and profile.

This information is used to authenticate you, display your signed-in account, and associate your account with MakeMyTube Pro subscription access when applicable.

YouTube Page and Activity Information

MakeMyTube processes information available on YouTube pages so that features such as recommendation filtering, saved videos, notes, progress tracking, trusted channels, and interface controls can function.

Depending on the features you use, this may include:

  • Video titles.
  • Video identifiers.
  • Channel names.
  • Video URLs.
  • Video duration.
  • Playback progress.
  • Watch time.
  • Focus Mode and Normal Mode activity.
  • Saved videos.
  • Timestamped notes created by you.
  • Interest or study tags.
  • Blocked tags.
  • Trusted-channel preferences.
  • Pomodoro session information.
  • Progress and productivity statistics.
  • Interactions required to operate MakeMyTube features.

This information is used only for MakeMyTube functionality and personalization.

2. Information Stored in Browser Storage

A significant portion of MakeMyTube preferences and activity data is stored using Chrome extension storage.

This may include:

  • MakeMyTube settings.
  • Recommendation mode preferences.
  • Interest or study tags.
  • Blocked tags.
  • Trusted channels.
  • Saved videos.
  • Timestamped notes.
  • Pomodoro settings and statistics.
  • Watch-time statistics.
  • Progress information.
  • Recommendation preferences.
  • Subscription status cached for the extension interface.

This information helps MakeMyTube remember your preferences and maintain your experience across browser sessions.

3. Local Import and Export

MakeMyTube may allow you to manually export certain supported information, such as saved-video records or progress information, into a file stored on your device.

You may later import a compatible MakeMyTube backup file to restore supported information after reinstalling the extension or moving to another supported browser environment.

Exported backup files are created only when you choose to export them. MakeMyTube does not automatically upload those exported files to a cloud storage service.

You are responsible for protecting any backup files that you create and store locally.

4. MakeMyTube Backend

MakeMyTube uses backend infrastructure hosted using Cloudflare Workers and related Cloudflare services for authentication and subscription management.

The backend may process or store information such as:

  • Your Google account identifier.
  • Your email address.
  • Your name.
  • Your profile picture URL, when available.
  • Your MakeMyTube subscription plan.
  • Your subscription status.
  • Your subscription start information.
  • Your subscription expiry information.
  • Razorpay subscription identifiers.
  • Payment or subscription event identifiers needed to manage subscription events.

This information is used to authenticate users and manage MakeMyTube account and subscription access.

5. Payments and Razorpay

MakeMyTube Pro subscriptions are processed using Razorpay.

When you choose to purchase MakeMyTube Pro, you may be redirected to Razorpay's payment interface.

MakeMyTube does not directly collect or store your credit card number, debit card number, UPI PIN, CVV, banking password, or similar sensitive payment credentials.

Razorpay processes payment information according to its own privacy and security practices.

MakeMyTube receives only information needed to determine and maintain subscription status, which may include Razorpay subscription identifiers, payment or subscription status, and billing-period information.

6. Google Sign-In

MakeMyTube uses Google OAuth for authentication.

Google Sign-In is used to:

  • Verify your identity.
  • Create or identify your MakeMyTube account.
  • Display basic account information inside the extension.
  • Associate your account with MakeMyTube Pro access when applicable.

MakeMyTube does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, Google Contacts, or other unrelated Google account content.

7. Chrome Extension Permissions

MakeMyTube requests Chrome permissions needed to provide its extension functionality.

Storage

Used to save MakeMyTube preferences, selected tags, saved videos, notes, progress information, Pomodoro data, and account or subscription state.

Identity

Used to support Google Sign-In and authentication.

Alarms

Used to support Pomodoro focus and break timers even when the extension popup is closed.

Declarative Net Request

Used for MakeMyTube's optional advertisement-reduction functionality through packaged browser rules.

YouTube Host Access

MakeMyTube needs access to YouTube pages so that it can provide recommendation filtering, interface customization, saved-video tools, notes, progress tracking, trusted-channel features, distraction controls, and related functionality.

MakeMyTube Backend Access

Access to the MakeMyTube backend is used for authentication and subscription management.

8. How We Use Information

Information accessed by MakeMyTube is used only for purposes connected to extension functionality, including:

  • Authenticating users.
  • Providing recommendation modes.
  • Filtering content according to user-selected preferences.
  • Applying blocked tags.
  • Prioritizing trusted channels.
  • Saving user preferences.
  • Providing saved-video functionality.
  • Providing timestamped notes.
  • Tracking supported progress and usage information.
  • Providing Pomodoro functionality.
  • Managing MakeMyTube Pro subscriptions.
  • Maintaining extension reliability.
  • Preventing duplicate or incorrect subscription processing.

9. Data Sharing

MakeMyTube does not sell user data.

MakeMyTube does not transfer user data to third parties for advertising profiling, creditworthiness, lending, or unrelated purposes.

Information may be processed by service providers when required to operate MakeMyTube, including:

  • Google — for Google OAuth authentication.
  • Cloudflare — for backend infrastructure, networking, database or storage services, and security.
  • Razorpay — for payment and subscription processing.

These service providers process information according to their own applicable privacy policies and terms.

10. Advertising

MakeMyTube does not use your personal information to create advertising profiles and does not sell your information to advertisers.

MakeMyTube may include optional features that attempt to reduce or hide some advertising-related elements or requests on YouTube.

These features do not involve selling or sharing user information for advertising purposes.

11. Remote Code

MakeMyTube does not download and execute remotely hosted JavaScript as extension application logic.

The extension may communicate with external services such as the MakeMyTube backend, Google authentication services, Razorpay, and YouTube, but executable extension logic is packaged with the Chrome extension.

12. Data Retention

Data stored in Chrome extension storage may remain there until it is cleared, reset, overwritten, removed through MakeMyTube controls, or removed according to Chrome's extension-storage behavior.

Account and subscription information stored through MakeMyTube's backend may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Maintain account access.
  • Provide subscription access.
  • Process subscription cancellation.
  • Process subscription expiration.
  • Prevent duplicate payment-event processing.
  • Meet applicable operational or legal requirements.

13. User Choices and Data Deletion

You can change or remove many types of locally stored MakeMyTube information through extension controls.

Depending on available features, this may include preferences, selected tags, blocked tags, saved videos, notes, recommendation information, or other locally stored activity information.

You may uninstall MakeMyTube from Chrome at any time.

14. Security

MakeMyTube uses reasonable technical measures intended to protect information handled by the service.

Examples may include:

  • HTTPS communication.
  • Google OAuth authentication.
  • Server-side payment API credentials.
  • Razorpay webhook signature verification.
  • Restricted Chrome extension host permissions.
  • Server-side subscription verification.

No online system can guarantee absolute security, but MakeMyTube is designed to avoid unnecessary collection and exposure of user information.

15. Children's Privacy

MakeMyTube is a general-purpose browser customization and productivity extension.

It is not specifically directed toward children under the minimum age required to independently use Google accounts, payment services, or Chrome services in their jurisdiction.

16. Third-Party Services

MakeMyTube interacts with third-party platforms and services, including YouTube, Google, Cloudflare, and Razorpay.

Use of those services may also be governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

MakeMyTube is an independent browser extension and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by YouTube or Google unless explicitly stated otherwise.
